About MediPredict

The story, model, and technology behind this application.

Project Overview

MediPredict is a machine learning web application that estimates the likelihood of five health conditions — Healthy, Pre-Diabetes, Hypertension, Heart Disease, and Diabetes — based on common patient vitals such as age, BMI, blood pressure, glucose level, cholesterol, heart rate, physical activity, and family history.

The underlying model is a Logistic Regression classifier trained on a dataset of 10,000 patient records. The full pipeline, from raw data to a trained model, is reproducible via a single training script.

How It Works

  1. Enter your health metrics into the prediction form.
  2. The app encodes your inputs exactly as the model was trained.
  3. The trained Logistic Regression model generates a prediction.
  4. You see the predicted condition and the model's confidence score.

Technologies Used

  • Backend: Python, Flask
  • Machine Learning: scikit-learn, pandas, numpy
  • Model Persistence: joblib
  • Frontend: HTML5, Jinja2, CSS3 (no frameworks)

Important Disclaimer

MediPredict is built for educational and demonstrative purposes only. Predictions generated by the model are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider with any questions about a medical condition.
